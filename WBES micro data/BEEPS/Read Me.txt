This database combines the World Bank Enterprise Surveys (WBES) included in the 2019-2020 rollout in Europe and Central Asia (ECA) and the Middle East and North Africa (MENA). The 41 economies included are listed below. 
All surveys used the same questionnaire, which included the rather lengthy Green Economy Module. The Morocco survey had additional Morocco-specific variables, which were dropped from this harmonized database. 
Some variables have been dropped to protect the anonymity of the respondents.

We ask that all users of the data to cite the data as follows - Source: World Bank Enterprise Surveys, http://www.enterprisesurveys.org
We also suggest that all users include an acknowledgment line in their papers or books.  We have provided suggested wording below, but please feel free to amend as appropriate:
"We thank the Enterprise Analysis Unit of the Development Economics Global Indicators Department of the World Bank Group for making the data available."
